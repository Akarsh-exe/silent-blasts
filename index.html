Silent Blasts

A lightweight, client-side Minecraft mod that filters specific blast sound events. Reduces audio clutter during fast gameplay without affecting gameplay mechanics, hitboxes, or anything server-side.

What it does

● Filters blast sounds — mutes specific sound events locally on your client.
● Client-side only — no server changes, no gameplay effects, no hitbox or damage changes.
● Lightweight — small jar, minimal performance impact, no dependencies beyond Fabric API.

Requirements

Minecraft 1.21.11

Side Client

Fair Play & Compliance

● No unfair advantage — This mod only filters sound output on your client. It does not reveal hidden information, automate gameplay, alter hitboxes, change damage, or modify any server-side mechanics.
● Client-side only — Servers and other players cannot detect it. It has no effect on gameplay and cannot be used to gain information a player wouldn't otherwise have.
● Allowed on major PvP servers — Leg.GG's modification guidelines explicitly list "Any sound muffler mods" as allowed. OGCraft's mod policy confirms: "If a mod does not give you an unfair advantage over other players, it is allowed."

Verified by third-party rules and official terms:

→ Minecraft End-User License Agreement (EULA) → Leg.GG Modification Guidelines — "Any sound muffler mods" allowed → OGCraft Client Mod Policy — "No unfair advantage" rule

Installation

Install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 1.21.11
Download and drop Fabric API into your mods/ folder
Download silent-blasts-1.0.0.jar and place it in the same mods/ folder
Launch Minecraft with the Fabric profile — done
